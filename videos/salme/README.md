# The Viking Ships Beneath Salme

90 original landscape PNG exports for the Salme history video, numbered `001_duration 1s_00-00-00-01.png` through `090_duration 5.404s_09-08-09-13.404.png`. Every image is **1920 × 1080 pixels (16:9)**. The earlier nine-image revision remains, and images 032–040 have now been regenerated to clarify the burial sequence.

The imagery uses the cartoon short skill and the reference-based design guide. Captions, dates, counts and hypothetical-scene labels are left for video assembly. At the user’s request, maps and schematic route marks are now included in 026, 052 and 069. The 082–083 shoe transition requires crop alignment. Historical images are illustrative reconstructions; the archaeological schematics are not measured plans.

## Image filenames and corrected audio timing

The scene filenames now use `number_duration Xs_start-end`, for example `005_duration 4s_00-08-00-12.png`. The time ranges follow the supplied narration transcript rather than the original nine-minute edit plan. Colons are replaced with hyphens for Windows compatibility.

See [image-durations.csv](image-durations.csv), [the timing review](TIMING_REVIEW.md), and [full image/transcript matches](transcript-image-timing.json). The original audio runs to 09:13.404; the first three images use documented opening inserts. The previous nine-minute preview and rendered MP4 have not been retimed by this rename.
